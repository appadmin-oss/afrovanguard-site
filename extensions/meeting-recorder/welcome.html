Set up the Meeting Recorder

Two steps, once. After that, open a meeting and press Alt+Shift+R.

1. Let it hear your microphone

Chrome asks once. Without it, only the other people in the call are recorded.

2. Connect your Afrovanguard account

So each recording lands on the right meeting and its minutes are written for you.

Recordings are sent only to your organisation’s portal. Audio is deleted after the minutes are written and the retention period passes; the minutes stay on the meeting.
