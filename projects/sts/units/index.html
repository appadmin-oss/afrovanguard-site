Operating units · 7

Seven institutional units. Each with one job.

STS is organised around seven units that map onto the work itself. The list below shows each unit's purpose, three or four responsibilities, and the named lead.

01 Programs Unit 02 Research & Measurement 03 Partner Schools Unit 04 Safeguarding 05 Communications & Media 06 Finance & Operations 07 Fundraising & Partnerships
Unit 01 / 07

Programs Unit

Designs, runs and reports on the four program streams.

  • Curriculum design across all four streams
  • Cohort intake, attendance, retention
  • Termly baseline–endline assessment cycle
  • Public reporting per cohort
Lead
Mariam Olatunji
Unit 02 / 07

Research & Measurement

Owns methodology, assessment instruments and data publication.

  • Maintain baseline–endline framework
  • Publish raw data with every annual report
  • Peer-review of internal findings
Lead
Chinwe Adeyemi
Unit 03 / 07

Partner Schools Unit

Manages the 23 partner-school relationships.

  • Per-LGA partner liaisons
  • MOU renewal and safeguarding compliance
  • On-site STS facilitator placement
Lead
Olumide Ojo
Unit 04 / 07

Safeguarding

Child-protection oversight across every program and volunteer interaction.

  • Volunteer vetting and DBS-equivalent checks
  • Incident logging and Council escalation
  • Annual safeguarding policy review
Lead
Ngozi Eze
Unit 05 / 07

Communications & Media

Public-facing voice, blog, donor reporting.

  • Editorial calendar for field notes and methodology briefs
  • Photography and consent-form stewardship
  • Quarterly newsletter
Lead
Yetunde Lawal
Unit 06 / 07

Finance & Operations

Books, budgets, audits, logistics.

  • Cost ledger maintenance (the basis of all sponsor impact statements)
  • Monthly management accounts
  • Annual external audit
Lead
Funke Adeyinka
Unit 07 / 07

Fundraising & Partnerships

Sponsor, partner and donor pipeline.

  • Institutional partnership intake (4-branch flow)
  • Sponsor onboarding and termly reporting
  • Donor stewardship
Lead
Tobi Aremu
