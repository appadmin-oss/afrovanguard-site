What to expect

Three things every pathway has in common.

01
An honest preview
Each pathway shows you what the next steps look like, how long they take, and what we'll ask of you, before you sign anything.
02
A real human
Your application or enquiry is reviewed by a named member of staff, not a queue. We aim to reply within two business days.
03
Closure either way
We tell you when you're in, when you're not, and when we need to take another look. Silence is never the answer.
