How we work

Five pillars. The same across every program.

We're not the only ones doing education work in Lagos. We try to be among the most transparent. The five-step rhythm below applies whether the cohort is 12 or 412.

01
Diagnose
Baseline assessments at intake. We measure what we plan to change before we change it.
02
Design
Curriculum and session plans built with practising teachers from the partner schools we serve.
03
Deliver
Cohorts capped at 24. Twice-weekly sessions. Attendance and engagement tracked per child.
04
Document
Endline assessments at the end of every term. Methods, raw data and findings published openly.
05
Distribute
Reports shared with partner schools, funders, and the public — including the parts that didn't work.
Evidence
We collect baseline and endline data on every cohort. When something works, we say by how much. When it doesn't, we say so and revise.
