LCASP 2026

Executive Summary

📅 Commencement: Monday, October 25, 2025

The Lagos Community Advancement School Project (LCASP) is a bold escalation of the Street-To-Stardom Project under Afrovanguard. It is designed to tackle corruption, civic decay, and cultural erosion from the root — the grassroots.

Across Lagos, children face poor civic education, declining communal support, and weak cultural identity. If this continues, corruption will remain recycled from generation to generation. LCASP intervenes early, raising a generation equipped with civic responsibility, cultural consciousness, and leadership values.

Through schools, volunteers, and community hubs (CACENTREs), LCASP will mobilize trained volunteers to educate and mentor 600 children across 6 Alimosho LGA communities.

Problem Statement

Why LCASP
Exists

Corruption in Nigeria is not only a governance failure — it is a cultural and civic failure rooted in the absence of foundational values education. Children growing up today are rarely taught integrity, civic duty, or communal accountability.

📚

Civic Education Gap

Nigeria's school curriculum lacks robust civic and anti-corruption education at the secondary level, leaving millions of future leaders without foundational values frameworks.

🏘️

Fractured Communities

Urban migration and economic pressure have eroded the communal parenting structures that once allowed communities to shape children's character collectively.

🌱

Early Intervention Window

Research shows that values and civic identity are most effectively formed between ages 10–16. LCASP targets this precise developmental window.

The Strategy

Why Grassroot?

Systemic change requires cultural change — and culture is formed at the community level.

🌍 Cultural Backing

Pre-colonial African societies practiced communal parenting — elders, neighbors, and relatives shaped children's values. This system was disrupted, leaving parents isolated and children exposed to negative influences.

LCASP restores this structure. When communities take responsibility for their children, corruption at the grassroots has nowhere to grow.

🎯 SDG Escalation

By embedding civic responsibility and cultural values early, LCASP helps Lagos State accelerate achievement of key global development goals:

  • SDG 4: Access to quality civic and cultural education
  • SDG 10: Leveling opportunity gaps across communities
  • SDG 11: Building sustainable, cohesive communities
  • SDG 16: Cultivating leaders with justice and integrity
  • SDG 17: Engaging in sustainable partnerships

What LCASP Does

Five Objectives.
One Clear Mission.

Every initiative within LCASP maps back to one of five specific, measurable goals for Lagos State communities.

01

Educate

Provide structured Leadership and Community Advancement training for Lagos secondary school students at 6 community centres.

02

Mobilize

Raise and train volunteers to mentor children and support communities across Alimosho LGA throughout all four programme episodes.

03

Establish

Create and strengthen Community Advancement Centres (CACENTREs) in Egbeda, Ayobo, and four other communities for permanent, continuous learning.

04

Prevent

Reduce juvenile delinquency and grassroots corruption through values-based early intervention starting at age 10.

05

Unite

Promote collaboration between parents, teachers, and communities to restore the African communal parenting tradition.

Case Study — Ayobo 2025

After just four weeks of LCASP at the Ayobo centre in 2025, parents rallied together and demanded the work not end. They formally requested a permanent Community Advancement Centre (CACENTRE) — just like the Egbeda model. When communities take ownership, sustainability is inevitable.
