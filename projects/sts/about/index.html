About

A measured, evidence‑led NGO working where Lagos overlooks.

Street-To-Stardom is the education and youth-development arm of Afrovanguard. We run four programs across four Lagos LGAs, with cohorts capped at 24 and outcomes published every term.

01 · Origin
Founded 2019. Registered NGO. Operating in Alimosho, Ikeja, Kosofe, Agege.

We began in 2019 in a single rented classroom in Alimosho. The premise was unfashionable: that the gap between under-resourced public schools and the children who attended them was not a literacy problem, but a logistics one — that the teaching could be excellent, the kids could be eager, and yet entire cohorts were leaving primary school unable to read at grade level.

Six years later we are embedded in twenty-three schools, with four programs and a habit of publishing what we measure. We are still small. We are still local. We are not in a rush to be either of those things less.

The point of this work is not to design beautiful programs. It is to find out which combinations of structure, attention and time produce a child who can read. — Operating note, Council minutes, March 2023

What we are, plainly

Four programs. One framework. A staff of nine, a council of seven, two hundred and thirty-four active volunteers. We do not run a school. We run with schools, around schools, after school. Our cohort sizes are deliberately small. Our reporting is deliberately granular.

What we are not

We are not a charity in the giving-tin sense. Every shilling we receive is reported against a published cost ledger; the ratio of programmatic to overhead spend appears in our annual report and again in the audited financials. We are not a research institute, but we run our programs with a research-institute's appetite for measurement.

Vision & mission

Two sentences. Six years of work behind each.

Vision · 2030

A Lagos in which every child has documented access to a literate education by the end of primary school.

"Documented" is the operative word. We don't aim to be the largest provider. We aim to be the most transparent — and to push everyone else in our field toward the same standard.

Mission · today

Run small, measurable, replicable programs that close documented learning gaps in under-resourced communities.

Cohorts of 24 maximum. Baseline-endline data on every child. Methodology and findings published openly, including the parts that didn't work.

Our relationship with Afrovanguard

Street-To-Stardom is one of three operating initiatives under Afrovanguard, the parent organisation through which our governance, finance and safeguarding obligations are administered. Afrovanguard provides the institutional scaffolding; STS does the program work.

In practice this means a shared Board of Trustees, a shared safeguarding policy, and a single audited set of financial statements that breaks out our share of the budget line by line. The two brands are kept visually distinct so that supporters always know who is doing what.
