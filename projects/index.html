Getting Started

Your Path into
the Movement

Most programs are free. Registration is open. The only qualification is a genuine desire to lead and serve.

1

Discover your program

Browse the grid above. Find the one that matches your gifting, season, and goals — arts, tech, leadership, or faith.

2

Register or apply

Most are free with open registration. Selective programs (NGG, Academy) require a short application. All welcome Alimosho youth.

3

Show up and transform

Engage fully. Graduate as a certified Vanguard with real skills, renewed character, and a community built for the long haul.
