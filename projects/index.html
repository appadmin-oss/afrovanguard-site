Getting started

Your Path into the Movement

Most of our work is free. Registration is open. The only qualification is a genuine desire to lead and serve.

  1. 1

    Find your project

    Find the one that matches your gifting, season, and goals — arts, tech, leadership, or faith.

  2. 2

    Register or apply

    Most are free with open registration. The selective ones ask for a short application.

  3. 3

    Show up and transform

    Engage fully. Graduate as a certified Vanguard with real skills, renewed character, and a community built for the long haul.

Scaling the model

Bring Afrovanguard to Your Community

Every programme is delivered through the Afrovanguard Social Franchise — Level C leaders who establish Community Advancement Centres and run our flagship projects under one vision and one standard.

Franchise a CACENTREHow advancement works
  1. 1

    Qualify

    Reach Level C through verified output and leadership multiplication — bringing and mentoring committed members.

  2. 2

    Establish

    Open a financially sustainable CACENTRE with approved systems, curriculum, branding and the LCASP 12-month cycle.

  3. 3

    Operate to standard

    Run flagship projects to one culture and one standard — approval and oversight remain with Afrovanguard.
