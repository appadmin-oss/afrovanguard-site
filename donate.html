Questions & Answers

Your Donation, Answered

Is my donation tax-deductible? +
Yes. Afrovanguard is a registered nonprofit in Nigeria. Donations may be eligible for tax deductions under applicable law. We recommend consulting your tax advisor. We'll provide a donation receipt for all gifts above ₦5,000 or $5 USD.
How is my money used? +
100% of every donation goes directly to program delivery. Afrovanguard operates with a zero-admin-fee policy for public donations — meaning nothing is taken for staff salaries or admin from what you give. Our operational costs are covered by specific grants and institutional partnerships.
Is my payment secure? +
Absolutely. All card payments are processed by Paystack, a PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateway. Your card details are never stored on our servers. All transactions use 256-bit SSL encryption, and you may be prompted for 3D Secure authentication by your bank.
Can I give in USD or GBP from abroad? +
Yes. We accept donations in Nigerian Naira (NGN), US Dollars (USD), and British Pounds (GBP). Simply toggle the currency selector in the donation form before entering your amount. For wire transfers in USD or GBP, please contact [email protected] for international bank details.
Can I set up a recurring monthly donation? +
Yes! Select "Monthly" in the frequency tabs above the amount selector. Your card will be charged automatically each month until you cancel. Monthly donors receive quarterly impact reports showing exactly how their cumulative gift is making a difference. You can cancel at any time by emailing us.
What if I want to donate directly to a specific program? +
Simply click on the campaign card you want to support before completing your donation. Your gift will be tagged to that specific program and you'll receive program-specific impact updates. If your chosen program is fully funded, we'll contact you before redirecting your gift.
