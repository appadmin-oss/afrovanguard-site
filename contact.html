FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about contacting and working with Afrovanguard.

We respond within 24–48 hours on business days (Monday–Friday, 9 am–5 pm WAT). Emails sent on weekends or public holidays are picked up the next business day. For urgent matters, WhatsApp is fastest.
Afrovanguard operates two CACENTRE locations in Alimosho LGA, Lagos State, Nigeria: CACENTRE Ayobo and CACENTRE Egbeda. Use the location toggle in the map section above to switch between them.
Use the contact form above, select “Volunteering” as your purpose, and include your skills, availability, and current location. Remote mentorship is available. We especially welcome educators, mentors, tech professionals, coaches, and creatives.
Yes. We welcome partnerships with NGOs, corporations, government agencies, foundations, and academic institutions. Contact us with your organisation name, a brief proposal, and the value you bring. For formal discussions, email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng directly.
For donations above NGN 500,000 or corporate gifts, email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng first so we can arrange a call, send a formal receipt, and direct your gift properly. For individual donations, visit afrovanguard.org.ng/donate.
First check your spam folder. If 48 hours have passed on a business day, reach us via WhatsApp at +234 903 777 6318, or email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng with subject line “Follow-up: [your name]”.
