FAQs

Common Questions

Everything you need to know about contacting and working with Afrovanguard.

We respond within 24–48 hours on business days (Monday–Friday, 9 am–5 pm WAT). Messages sent on weekends or public holidays are picked up the next business day. For urgent matters, WhatsApp is fastest.

Afrovanguard operates two CACENTRE locations in Alimosho LGA, Lagos State, Nigeria: CACENTRE Ayobo and CACENTRE Egbeda. Use the location toggle above to switch between them.

Use the form above, select “Volunteering” as your purpose, and include your skills, availability, and location. Remote mentorship is available. We especially welcome educators, mentors, tech professionals, coaches, and creatives.

Yes. We welcome partnerships with NGOs, corporations, government agencies, foundations, and academic institutions. Send your organisation name, a brief proposal, and the value you bring — or email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng directly.

For donations above NGN 500,000 or corporate gifts, email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng first so we can arrange a call, send a formal receipt, and direct your gift properly. For individual donations, visit the donate page.

First check your spam folder. If 48 hours have passed on a business day, reach us on WhatsApp at +234 903 777 6318, or email contact@afrovanguard.org.ng with the subject “Follow-up: [your name]”.
