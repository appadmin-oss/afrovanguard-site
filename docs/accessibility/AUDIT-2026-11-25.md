# Accessibility audit — 25 November 2026

Site-wide audit of afrovanguard.org.ng against **WCAG 2.2 Level AA**, followed by
one round of fixes that turned the accessibility features into one system. The
public statement built on it is `/accessibility/` (`accessibility/index.php`):
**partially compliant**. Nothing here or there claims compliance.

Owner's brief: "improve, modernize and make the accessibility features industry
standard, screen reader-optimised, sleek and not patchy or disjointed."

## 1. What "industry standard" means here

| Source | What we took from it |
|---|---|
| **WCAG 2.2 AA** (W3C) | The target for every page. |
| **EN 301 549 v3.2.1** — the EU harmonised standard behind the European Accessibility Act (enforceable 28 June 2025); it points at WCAG 2.1 AA, and the coming v4.1.1 moves to WCAG 2.2 ([Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/eu-accessibility-requirements-and-eaa-compliance/), [UsableNet](https://blog.usablenet.com/understanding-wcag-and-en-301-549)) | WCAG 2.2 AA covers it; the statement names it. |
| **WebAIM Screen Reader User Survey #10** (2024, 1,539 respondents): 71.6% navigate long pages by headings; landmark use rose to 31.8%; missing or improper headings and too many links are among the most problematic items ([WebAIM](https://webaim.org/projects/screenreadersurvey10/)) | One `<h1>` per page and a real outline; the same named landmarks on every template; quieter footer (labelled groups rather than ten more headings). |
| **GOV.UK** — components tested with common screen reader/browser pairs; automated tools catch only ~30% of issues; the model accessibility statement ([design system](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/accessibility-statement), [service manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/technology/accessibility-for-developers-an-introduction), [DWP AT testing](https://accessibility-manual.dwp.gov.uk/best-practice/assistive-technology-testing)) | Statement rewritten on the model; error summary pattern; we say plainly that AT testing by users has not been done. |
| **W3C ARIA Authoring Practices** — native HTML first; the documented dialog, combobox, tabs and disclosure patterns ([APG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/)) | One native `<dialog>` implementation; IQ tabs rebuilt to the tabs pattern; palette stays the combobox pattern. |
| **FTC v. accessiBe** (January 2025) — overlays that claim to fix accessibility are not industry standard | The display settings stay conveniences on top of pages that must work without them; the wording says so; no "compliant" badge or claim anywhere. |

## 2. Method

* **Inventory crawler** (scratchpad `axe/inv.js`): every template family, signed out,
  as a learner and as a member (tokens from `tests/a11y/ci-session.php`, `CI=true`, in
  the test copy). Per page: landmarks (banner/main/contentinfo counts, nav names),
  heading outline and skipped levels, skip link and its target, `<title>`, `lang`,
  breadcrumb pattern, positive `tabindex`, `<img>` without `alt`, exposed icons in
  controls, `aria-label` on generic elements, live-region count, personal-data
  fields without `autocomplete`, unlabelled fields, vague link text, tables
  without captions, horizontal overflow; then **axe-core 4.10** (WCAG 2.0–2.2 A/AA
  plus best-practice). At 1280px and 320px.
* **Accessibility tree**: Playwright `locator.ariaSnapshot()` of Home, the
  accessibility page, an Academy course, a lesson and the portal — read as a screen
  reader is given them.
* **Manual review** of the shared scripts and styles (dialogs, focus rings,
  announcers, shortcuts), the forms, and the statement.
* **Settings on**: axe and reflow again with 200% text + spacing + high contrast
  (light and dark), at both widths.
* **Focus not obscured** (2.4.11): Tab through each page and check that no fixed or
  sticky element hides the focused control completely.

Not done, and not possible here: testing with real screen readers (NVDA, JAWS,
VoiceOver, TalkBack, Narrator) by the people who use them, voice control,
switch access. See §7.

## 5. Unified system

**`assets/site/a11y-core.js` (`window.AvA11y`)** — loaded first on every page
(`A11y::head()`, `lib/errors.php`, the static pages):

* `dialog()` — native `<dialog>` + `showModal()` (inert page, Escape), focus in,
  Tab round, backdrop closes, no page scroll, focus back to the opener or a
  fallback. Used by the **settings panel** (a11y.js) and the **search palette**
  (avsearch.js).
* `announce()` — one polite and one assertive region per page; moves into the open
  modal so it is heard. Used by the panel, the palette (result counts, Chioma
  answers), **Listen**, the **People** filter count.
* `trap()` — the shared Tab round, also used by **Chioma**'s phone sheet.
* `disclosure()`, `reducedMotion()`, `singleKeys()`, `typing()`, `modalOpen()`
  (Chioma's Escape yields to an open modal), `errorSummary()` (GOV.UK pattern,
  used by the **contact form**).

**Tokens (`assets/site/a11y.css` `:root`)** — `--av-ring` (one blue ring, 3px,
applied on every template; black/yellow in high contrast), `--av-target` (44px),
`--av-ui-*` (surface, ink, radius, shadow), `--av-motion` (0s under reduced motion
or "Stop moving content"). The panel, Listen, the search palette and the hub cards
share them: same radius, soft surfaces with no bordered rows, one accent (ink),
one icon grid (24px, 1.8 stroke), one entrance animation.

**Templates** — one skip link wording; one title pattern; named landmarks
(Main, Breadcrumb, Footer, Sections…); one breadcrumb pattern.

**Keyboard shortcuts**, documented in one table on `/accessibility/#shortcuts`:
Alt/Option+Shift+A (settings), Ctrl/⌘+K (search; the portal's own palette in the
portal), `/` (search, off-switchable), ↑/↓/Enter (palette), ←/→ (tabs, chips),
Esc (close; stop Listen). Alt+Shift+A and Ctrl+K do not collide with NVDA
(Insert/CapsLock), JAWS or VoiceOver (Ctrl+Option) modifiers; single-letter quick
navigation keys are consumed by the screen reader in browse mode, and `/` is
switchable for focus/forms mode and speech users.

## 6. Tests and CI

* `tests/a11y/axe-ci.js` — every template family (incl. lesson player, learner
  pages, portal views, constitution, sign-in, error pages, offline), 1280 and
  320, settings off and on (200% + spacing + high contrast light/dark), palette
  open (empty, results) and panel open on five chromes; fails on any WCAG 2.2
  A/AA axe violation, page overflow at 320px, or focus obscured.
* `tests/a11y/tree-ci.js` — accessibility-tree outline snapshots (landmarks + h1)
  of Home, the hub, an Academy course, a lesson and the portal, and the full panel
  and palette trees (`tests/a11y/snapshots/`).
* `tests/templatecontract.test.php` — renders each template through the router
  and checks lang, one h1, one main, banner/contentinfo, skip link + target,
  title format, breadcrumbs, no positive tabindex, img alt, named buttons/links.
* `tests/a11y/launchers-ci.js` — adds the shared announcer and the single-key
  switch.

Results of this round: see §8.

## 7. What remains (also in the statement)

* **Real assistive-technology testing by disabled people** — NVDA + Firefox/Chrome,
  JAWS + Chrome, VoiceOver on iOS and macOS, TalkBack on Android, Narrator; voice
  control (Voice Access, Dragon), switch access, ZoomText/magnifiers. Not done.
  Automated checks find roughly a third of issues.
* **Older Academy videos without captions/transcripts** (1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.2.5) —
  target 31 March 2027.
* **PDFs** (annual reports) not tagged (1.3.1, 2.4.2) — accessible versions on request.
* **Staff tools** (Studio, admin, NGV desk and staff pages, printable cards and
  posters): sign-in screens fixed (main, h1, contrast); the desks still lack a skip
  link and landmarks; staff email fields are other people's addresses
  (`autocomplete` would be wrong there, 1.3.5 does not apply). Target 30 June 2027.
  The Academy Studio keeps its own `/` shortcut (not yet tied to the switch).
* **Error summary rollout** — done for the contact form; the NGV register shows one
  server message at a time (focused); the mentor application and Academy forms
  still use inline messages only.
* **Portal live regions** — 41 per page (one per form message, empty until used);
  a few lists (`oweList`, `trList`) are live and can be chatty. Consolidating them
  onto `AvA11y.announce()` is the next step.
* **Sign-in and offline** are single-task screens without site header/footer
  landmarks (by design; the contract test exempts them by name).
* **Card links** on Home and listings read as long names (the whole card); valid
  (2.4.4) but verbose — a candidate for the "headings + one link" card pattern.
* **Inline handlers left**: DNS page "copy link" box (`onfocus`), portal Google
  disconnect `onsubmit` confirm, staff/print pages.
