Our story

A Movement Born from Conviction

Afrovanguard began not with a grant or a boardroom decision — but with a conviction. In 2017, a small group of passionate Nigerians saw a continent overflowing with talent, yet starved of ethical leadership. They asked one question: what if we poured everything into young people, before the system corrupts them?

Starting in Alimosho — one of Lagos’s densest and most dynamic communities — Afrovanguard launched its first creative arts programme, bringing world-class mentorship, skills development, and character formation directly to young people who had never had access to it. Within two years, the ripple effect was undeniable.

Today we remain rooted in Alimosho while our programmes span creative arts, technology, media, faith formation, career development, and international opportunities. Our metric of success has never changed: the quality of character we develop, not just the number of lives we touch.

The Early Years

A movement to raise incorruptible leaders, rooted in one Lagos community.

Out of conviction, a founding vision

2017 · The founding

In 2017, a small group of Nigerians saw a continent overflowing with talent, yet starved of ethical leadership. Their question was simple: what if we poured everything into young people, before the system corrupts them?

They started in Alimosho — the most populous local government area in Lagos — with Street-To-Stardom, a creative arts programme that channels music, dance and performance into discipline, confidence and professional-grade careers.

From the start, the measure of success was character, not headcount: mentorship, skills and values delivered directly to young people who had never had access to them.

Born from STS, a home for exceptional talent

2019 · Next Generation Genius

Within two years the ripple effect was undeniable. Out of Street-To-Stardom came Next Generation Genius — a selective programme that accelerates exceptional young minds through competitive challenges, scholarships and residential programming.

By now more than 300 teenagers were being shaped every year, and the model was proving that depth in one community beats breadth across many.

An Era of Expansion

From the arts to technology, media and global opportunity — the movement widens its reach without leaving home.

Technology, media and the world beyond

2021 · Techome, MediaPro, Africa GATES

Techome began training African developers, designers and tech entrepreneurs from right here in Alimosho. MediaPro set out to develop journalists, videographers and digital storytellers who would shape how Africa is seen and heard.

Africa GATES opened the door to international scholarships and fellowships, walking applicants through every step — so the world’s best opportunities could actually reach African youth.

Eight programmes, one standard

2023 · 10,000+ lives

With the Business Executive Club, Career Hub and the Kingdom Advancement Project, Afrovanguard reached eight active programmes and more than 5,000 lives transformed — all born in one community.

School Storm took free tutoring, mentorship and motivation into secondary schools across Alimosho, and the Afrovanguard Academy began forming the Next Generation Vanguard cohort.

From a community to a model

2024 – 2026 · LCASP & CACENTRE

In December 2024 the Lagos Chapter of volunteers came together to plan the next phase. The answer was LCASP: a 12-month cycle delivered through Community Advancement Centres across Egbeda, Ayobo, Idimu, Ikotun, Mosan and Ijaiye.

In 2026 the campaign set out to reach 900 children across six centres and 30,000 students through School Storm — and the Social Franchise opened, so qualified leaders can run CACENTREs to one vision, one governance and one standard.

Toward one million leaders

2040 · The vision

The goal has never been bigger, or clearer: raise one million incorruptible African leaders by 2040 — people who cannot be bought, broken or bent, no matter the system, no matter the pressure.

Who we are

A Movement Built on Unbreakable Values

We are committed to raising a new kind of African — incorruptible, self-aware, and community-driven. Not driven by trends, noise, or external validation, but by values that shape identity, guide action, and sustain transformation.

01

Mission

To cultivate individuals and institutions that advance justice, accountability, cultural dignity, sustainable prosperity, community development, and responsible leadership — through education, service, innovation, advocacy, and ethical action.

02

Vision

To raise a generation of ethical, competent, culturally grounded, and socially responsible leaders who become a transformative force for good in every sphere of human endeavour.

03

Our Code

To be Afrovanguard is to live by a code — a commitment to personal excellence and collective advancement. It is choosing to become a force for good in your life, your community, and Africa at large.

“We are not just building people. We are building a standard.”

Our 7 core values

The Character of a Vanguard

Seven principles that form the uncompromising character of every Afrovanguard member.

  1. 01

    Individuation

    The pursuit of self-discovery, self-mastery, and purpose fulfilment.

  2. 02

    Faith

    A deep belief in our roots — our ancestry, our heritage and the generations who came before us — and the confidence to build on them.

  3. 03

    Diligence

    A consistent commitment to excellence, discipline, and productivity.

  4. 04

    Accountability

    Ownership of actions, responsibilities, decisions, and outcomes.

  5. 05

    Responsibility

    An active commitment to solving problems and advancing society.

  6. 06

    Cultural Appreciation

    Respecting, preserving, and advancing humanity’s diverse heritage.

  7. 07

    Communal Spirit

    Promoting cooperation, solidarity, collective prosperity, and cohesion.

The force for good

Our Ethos

Afrovanguardism is a values-driven civic, cultural, leadership and human-development movement. Our ethos binds every member through nine guiding commitments — from the dignity of the person to the stewardship of the earth.

  • Human dignity & the common good
  • Selfless service & social impact
  • Integrity & anti-corruption
  • Cultural dignity & heritage
  • Good governance & peace
  • Knowledge & the environment

How Afrovanguard works

Progressive Growth & Member Alignment

A structured, high-frequency progression system built on personal growth, absolute accountability, invisible service and alignment with the mission. Advancement is earned through verifiable output — never by length of membership.

  1. O

    Foundation Member

    Every member begins here — mentored, stress-tested for consistency, and executing at least one verified task each week.

  2. A

    Advancement

    Earn Level A by bringing two committed members through your referral link and mentoring them into the ecosystem.

  3. C

    Leadership

    Dues become mandatory and servant leadership — the ego filter — becomes the standard. Advancement stays earned.

  • Algorithmic advancement — output over tenure
  • Radical transparency — 100% open-ledger dues
  • The ego filter — invisible service first

Scaling the model

Franchise a CACENTRE

Competent leaders establish Community Advancement Centres and run our flagship projects — LCASP, BEC, Africa GATES and Street-To-Stardom — under one governance framework and one operational standard.

  1. 01
    Establish a CACENTRE

    Qualified Level C leaders earn the right to open a financially sustainable Community Advancement Centre.

  2. 02
    Run flagship projects

    Deliver programmes using approved systems, curriculum, branding and standards — the LCASP 12-month cycle at the core.

  3. 03
    Under one standard

    Every centre operates to the same vision, governance and culture, with approval and oversight from Afrovanguard.
